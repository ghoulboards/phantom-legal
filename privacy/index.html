Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026.

This explains what Phantom collects and how it is used.

Information we collect

  • Account info: your email address and password, handled by our authentication provider.
  • Profile: the username you choose.
  • Content you create: decks and the cards/leaders you select, plus matches you host or join and their scores, counters, and participants.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate the app — to sign you in, show your decks and match history, and keep live match state in sync between the players in a match.

Storage & processors

Data is stored using Supabase, our backend and database provider, on their infrastructure. Authentication and passwords are handled by that provider; we do not store your password in plain text.

Realtime presence

During a match, limited presence information (such as whether the host is currently connected) is shared with the participants of that match so the scoreboard stays in sync.

Camera & invites

  • Camera: used only to scan a match's QR code so you can join it. The camera feed is processed on your device and is never recorded, stored, or uploaded.
  • Text invites: if you invite someone by text message, the app opens your device's messaging app with a pre-filled message. We do not access your contacts or see the messages you send.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. We do not use third-party advertising or analytics tracking SDKs. We share data only with our infrastructure provider as needed to run the app, or where required by law.

Data retention & your choices

You can edit your profile and delete your decks and matches in the app. We keep your data while your account is active.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, decks, and match data. You can also request access to or deletion of your data by emailing theghxvl@gmail.com; we will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change when we do.

Contact
