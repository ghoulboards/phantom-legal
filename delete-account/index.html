Delete your Phantom account

Last updated October 20, 2026.

You can permanently delete your Phantom account and all data associated with it.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Phantom and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email theghxvl@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Phantom account". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and login credentials)
  • Your profile and username
  • Your decks
  • Matches you host or joined, including scores and counters

Nothing is kept after deletion, apart from anything we are legally required to retain.
